Privacy Policy

Overview

Steady stores your goals and entries on your device and syncs them with your private iCloud account when available. RoCo Works does not collect, sell, track, or share your personal data. Steady does not use third-party advertising or analytics.

Information Steady Stores

Steady stores the goals, entries, notes, dates, and related app data that you create while using the app. This information is stored locally on your device.

RoCo Works does not operate its own servers for Steady user data and does not receive the goals or entries you create in Steady.

iCloud Sync

When iCloud is available and enabled on your device, Steady may sync your app data through your private iCloud account so it can stay up to date across your Apple devices.

iCloud is provided by Apple. Data stored in or synced through iCloud is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Data Collection

RoCo Works does not collect personal data from Steady. We do not sell personal data, track you across apps or websites, or share your personal data with others.

Third-Party Services

Steady does not use third-party advertising or analytics services. The only third-party service involved in storing or syncing your Steady data is Apple iCloud, when iCloud is available and enabled.

Children’s Privacy

Steady is not designed to collect personal information from anyone, including children. RoCo Works does not knowingly collect personal data from children through Steady.

Changes to This Policy

RoCo Works may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact RoCo Works at support@rocoworks.com.
